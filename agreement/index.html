The notice

BoardRank is an independent and unofficial website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, operated by, or an official service of the Chattogram Education Board or any government authority.

Student results and related information displayed on BoardRank are derived from result information made publicly accessible through official examination-result sources. BoardRank does not claim ownership of the underlying examination records.

Rankings displayed on BoardRank are unofficial calculations created by BoardRank using the available result data. They are not official board rankings and should not be treated as certificates, official merit positions, or decisions of the education board.

BoardRank does not intentionally obtain information through unauthorized access to private systems. However, the availability of information from a public source does not itself imply endorsement of BoardRank's reuse or ranking methodology.

BoardRank processes only information reasonably necessary to provide result lookup, comparison, ranking, and related functionality. We do not intentionally publish passwords, financial information, contact information, government identification numbers, or other unrelated sensitive information.

If you are the student, parent or legal guardian, or otherwise authorized person and believe that information is inaccurate or should not be displayed, submit a correction or removal request at nishchup489@gmail.com or contact us through About us.

By using BoardRank, you acknowledge that the rankings are unofficial and may contain errors or incomplete information.
