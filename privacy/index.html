Prototype Privacy Notice

Privacy

This page outlines privacy considerations for the SSC Rank prototype and the future public ranking experience.

Unofficial Platform

SSC Rank is not affiliated with, issued by, or endorsed by any education board. Public pages may eventually display student names, examination roll numbers, schools, result marks, and ranking information.

Student Data

Some SSC candidates may be minors. The production site should use only the information needed for result display and ranking, avoid unnecessary tracking, and provide a clear correction or removal process. Contact details are not finalized yet.

Analytics

Lightweight analytics may be used to understand traffic and reliability. The project should avoid invasive tracking and should not add fake visitor counters or dynamic activity claims.

Corrections / Removal

A correction and removal request process will be defined before publishing real data. Placeholder: contact method to be finalized by the project owner.
